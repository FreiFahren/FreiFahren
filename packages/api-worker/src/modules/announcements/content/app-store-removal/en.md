Apple has removed FreiFahren from the App Store. You can still use it though.

## Already have the app?

It keeps working like before, nothing changes for you. Just don't delete it: once it's gone, you can't download it again.

## Use the web app

FreiFahren also runs in your browser at [app.freifahren.org](https://app.freifahren.org). Add it to your home screen and it works just like the app, with its own icon and without the browser bar.

- **iPhone:** open the site in Safari, tap the share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android:** open the site in Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), then "Add to Home screen".
